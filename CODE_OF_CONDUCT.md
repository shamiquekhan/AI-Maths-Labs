# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people can learn mathematics and
engineering without being mocked for what they do not yet know. Contributors and
maintainers pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be precise and kind when reviewing code or mathematics.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Ask clarifying questions before assuming bad intent.
- Respect differing levels of experience and different learning paces.
- Give credit for derivations, code, data, and ideas.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, publishing
private information without permission, and dismissive behaviour towards
learners are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments,
close issues, or restrict participation when behaviour violates this code.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
